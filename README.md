# E-Commerce-Sales-Intelligence-Dashboard

An e-commerce company needs a self-service analytics layer for its sales team. The business questions: Which product categories drive the most revenue? Which regions are underperforming? How does monthly repeat-purchase rate trend over 12 months? The job of this project: build the entire BI layer from raw CSVs to a polished, drillable report.

Learning Resources: Google Digital Marketing and E-Commerce Professional Certificate.
